I'm a **BBIS (Bachelor of Business Information Systems)** finalist at UMT, Lahore (Class of 2026), with a track record spanning **frontend development, business intelligence, enterprise ERP, and data operations**. - 🏢 **Enterprise Systems** — Full-cycle ERP implementation in Odoo and SAP (Financial & Supply Chain modules)
- 🎬 **Creative Suite** — End-to-end content production with Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro & After Effects

**Open to:** Frontend Engineering · BI & Data Analysis · ERP/CRM Implementation · Internships · Junior Roles
